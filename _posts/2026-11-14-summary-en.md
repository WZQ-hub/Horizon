### [Orcas Airband Monitor Channels and Transcribes Live Radio Traffic](https://orcasairband.com/) ⭐️ 7.0/10

An Orcas Island airband monitor provides live radio traffic and transcripts across aviation frequencies. Its creator says it uses an ETTUS B210 software-defined radio connected to a dual-RTX 4090 computer, with CUDA-based polyphase channelization to separate signals, demodulate them, detect transmissions and discard data traffic. The creator reported that local pilots found it useful for following chatter during a ground stop, although the supplied implementation description is truncated and transcript reliability is unclear.

hackernews · mmaunder · Nov 13, 22:04 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50490593)

**「How channelization works」** A software-defined radio captures radio signals for processing in software. A polyphase channelizer separates a broadband input into multiple narrower frequency bands, allowing individual channels to be processed separately.

**「Community discussion」** The creator shared unedited, sometimes unintelligible transcripts that may have related to the ground stop. Another commenter pointed to an exchange in which a controller confirmed that nothing was departing to land at Vancouver; these snippets do not independently establish the full scope or cause of the disruption.

### [ICML 2027 Reportedly Adds Two-Stage Review and Author Limits](https://www.reddit.com/r/MachineLearning/comments/1xvkxbj/icml_2027_changes_d/) ⭐️ 7.0/10

A Reddit post reporting on the ICML 2027 call for papers says the conference will use a two-stage review process: an area chair decides whether a paper proceeds to full review, and a senior area chair must confirm any proposed first-stage rejection. Authors would receive a short justification by February 23. The post also reports a 20-submission cap per author, a reciprocal-reviewer requirement capped at six papers per reviewer, and a program committee service requirement for anyone on three or more submissions. Papers that do not comply may be desk rejected.

reddit · r/MachineLearning · /u/Gravitist · Nov 13, 21:53

**「Background」** Reciprocal reviewing ties the supply of reviewers to paper submissions by requiring authors to contribute to the review process. A desk rejection removes a submission from consideration without a full review.

**「Implications for authors」** According to the post, each author may appear on at most one paper whose authors include no eligible reciprocal reviewer. Authors with multiple submissions may therefore need to coordinate reviewer eligibility across their coauthors as well as observe the submission and service limits.

### [TorchTitan Team Reports 1T MoE Training on 384 GH200 GPUs](https://www.reddit.com/r/MachineLearning/comments/1xvce5h/scaling_to_1t_moe_on_torchtitan_p/) ⭐️ 7.0/10

A contributor working with TorchTitan and PyTorch maintainers reports training an approximately one-trillion-parameter mixture-of-experts model on 384 NVIDIA GH200 GPUs at roughly 1,300 tokens per second per GPU. The post claims a 14-fold improvement over the initial setup, using 384-way FSDP and four-way expert parallelism kept within nodes. These are the authors’ results; the supplied post does not provide enough benchmark detail to independently assess the throughput or overall improvement.

reddit · r/MachineLearning · /u/f14-bertolotti · Nov 13, 16:28

**「How the model is distributed」** A mixture-of-experts model routes tokens to selected subsets of its parameters, or experts. The authors used FSDP to shard weights across GPUs and expert parallelism to distribute experts, avoiding pipeline parallelism because they favored shorter steps and faster iteration.

**「Practical trade-offs」** The post offers techniques for teams facing network and memory bottlenecks, including activation checkpointing, lower-precision communication and keeping expert matrices together for local optimizer updates. It also cautions that group-limited routing improved throughput by 7% in their runs but may hurt training, and that one allocator workaround uses an FSDP2 private flag that is only on its way to becoming public.

**Tags**: `#mixture-of-experts`, `#distributed training`, `#TorchTitan`, `#PyTorch`, `#NVIDIA GH200`
